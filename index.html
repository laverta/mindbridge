Built to understand, not to fix

A state parser, not a therapy program.

01

Understanding first

Help a person recognize their own mechanism. When they form their own strategy, affirm it or refine it lightly — never replace their agency with a prescription.

02

Metaphor safety

Every framework use is signed out loud: [agent metaphor] / [three-layer view] / [back to plain words]. The metaphor succeeds by naming experience; it fails by becoming a diagnosis.

03

Universal spectrum

Startup friction, overload, context drift — every brain enters these states. ADHD/AuDHD is simply the far end of the same spectrum. A universal tool, not a club.

Two failure modes

Coordination fails in two directions, and they are symmetric.

A

Noise collapse · dysregulated routing

Will-layer bandwidth runs out, the main agent loses the thread, sub-agents grab the bus: scattered thoughts, context drift, nothing starts.

B

Single-lens capture · over-routing

One sub-agent delivers early wins and gradually monopolizes scheduling, becoming the only lens (hyperfocus). Looks like strength; the cost is that problems needing another lens get answered by this one, with high confidence.

?

Calibration test

Any trusted lens must answer: when was this conclusion last disproven by reality? A lens that cannot name its own misses has a broken feedback loop, not high accuracy.

Fast enough for a chat, strict enough to stay auditable.
