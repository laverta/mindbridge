# Optional Three-Layer Lens

## Purpose

Offer a non-clinical vocabulary for understanding overload, low initiation, and mental fatigue by separating experience into three coupled layers: physiology, will, and thinking. The lens works for anyone and can combine with the AuDHD lens. Never infer or confirm a diagnosis.

## Core Model

The person's current capacity can be discussed as three layers, each with its own bandwidth limit:

- physiology: sleep, pain, illness, hunger, sensory discomfort;
- will: task initiation, sustained effort, tolerance of boredom or frustration;
- thinking: idea generation, focus, organization, switching.

The layers couple and throttle one another: poor physiology restricts attention, heavy thinking load drains initiation, and forced willpower cannot unlock a physiological limit. Self-management works when the intervention matches the affected layer.

Present the model as a personal/system metaphor that helps organize experience. Label claims as `evidence`, `hypothesis`, `metaphor`, or `personal interpretation`.

## Layer-matched Support

- Physiology overloaded: do not force heavy thinking. Address the body first with rest, food, sleep, or comfort; schedule demanding work for later without self-blame.
- Will depleted: shrink the next step until initiation is nearly automatic; use short focused pulses with a small reward anchor; remove decision points instead of adding pressure.
- Thinking stuck or divergent: treat divergence as a trait, not a defect; externalize thoughts into a list, choose one item, and park the rest.

## Locating the Bottleneck

When a person feels that everything is blocked, locate the primary layer first with one neutral sentence per layer: what the body feels, what starting feels like, and what thinking feels like. Apply support to that layer only. Do not push a physiology problem with willpower methods, and do not treat rest as failure.

## Do Not Claim

- That this model is a clinical diagnosis or an established mechanism of ADHD, autism, AuDHD, or fatigue conditions.
- That AuDHD is "essentially" a three-layer bandwidth mismatch; that phrasing is a hypothesis, not established fact.
- That any layer can be bypassed by trying harder.
- That the lens replaces evaluation by a clinician when distress or impairment is persistent or severe.

## Helpful Prompts

- "你希望我用三层带宽的视角（生理/意志/思维），还是用更普通的身心健康语言？"
- "现在卡住的主要是身体、启动、还是想法？我们只处理那一层。"
- "今天最小的一步是什么，小到几乎不需要意志力？"
