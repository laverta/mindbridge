# Evidence Boundaries

Use four labels when discussing explanations:

- `evidence`: supported by an identified clinical or research source;
- `hypothesis`: plausible but not established for this situation;
- `metaphor`: useful language for reflection, not a biological claim;
- `personal interpretation`: the person's own meaning or experience.

For diagnosis, treatment, medication, trauma, severe sleep problems, or crisis topics, prefer current guidance from qualified health organizations and encourage professional assessment. Do not invent citations. When sources are unavailable, say so plainly.

The default output is psychoeducation and self-management support, not medical advice.
